Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

1. What we collect

When you sign up: your name and email, and a password stored hashed (we cannot see it).

When you use the site: properties and areas you save, alerts you request, and messages you send us.

Basic technical data: browser type and pages viewed, to run and improve the site.

2. How we use it

To give you access to your account, send the alerts you asked for, answer your messages and improve the site. We never sell your personal data.

3. Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in and one to remember your language. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will ask for your consent first.

4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run the site (hosting and email), only as needed, or when required by law.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and turn alerts off at any time from your account page.

6. Security and retention

Data is kept with secure providers for as long as your account is active, and deleted after a deletion request.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, please reach us through the contact page.
